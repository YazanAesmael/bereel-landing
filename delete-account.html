Delete Your Account.

EffectiveJuly 19, 2026 Last updatedJuly 19, 2026 Contactadmin@bereel.app

This page explains how to delete your BeReel account and everything in it — from inside the app, or by email if you can't sign in. It also lists exactly what gets erased, what we keep, and how long it takes.

01 — Delete from the app

The fastest way, and the one we recommend.

If you can still sign in to BeReel, delete your account directly from the app:

  1. Open BeReel and make sure you're signed in.
  2. Tap your profile in the bottom navigation bar.
  3. Tap the settings gear in the top-right corner.
  4. Scroll down to the Account section.
  5. Tap Delete account.
  6. Confirm when we ask. This is the last step — after it, there's no undo.

You'll be signed out immediately, on every device. From that moment your profile, your reels and your comments stop being visible to anyone on BeReel.

02 — Request by email

If you've lost access to the app, or you want your data erased entirely.

Email admin@bereel.app with the subject "Delete my account", and include the @username on the account.

Send it from the email address the account was created with. For your security we only act on deletion requests that come from the account's own email address — otherwise anyone could delete someone else's account.

Use this route if you can't sign in, or if you want your data permanently erased from our systems rather than only removed from BeReel — see below for the difference. We handle these requests within 30 days and reply to confirm when it's done.

03 — What deleting removes

Everything that is you, and everything you made — gone from BeReel.

The moment you confirm, all of the following stops existing as far as BeReel and everyone on it is concerned:

  • Your profile — username, display name, bio and avatar.
  • Every reel you posted, along with its cover image.
  • Your comments and replies, everywhere they appear.
  • Your likes and follows, in both directions.
  • Your watch history and the view records tied to your account.
  • Your notification tokens — no device of yours can be reached again.
  • Your login — you are signed out everywhere and cannot sign back in.

Nobody can find you, open your profile, watch your reels, read your comments, or contact you. Your @username is released and becomes available to someone else.

04 — Removed from BeReel vs erased from our systems

We're being precise here, because the difference matters.

Deleting in the app deactivates your account permanently and removes all of it from BeReel. It is irreversible from your side — we will not reactivate a deleted account. What it does not do, on its own, is wipe the underlying records from our database and media storage; those are retained internally, unreachable by you or any other user, for safety, anti-abuse and legal-compliance reasons.

If you want your data erased outright — the account row, the stored video and image files, and everything attached to them — say so in your email. We'll carry out a full erasure and confirm when it's finished.

Either way, we never sell your data, and we never use a deleted account's content to build a profile of you or to train anything.

05 — How long it takes

Immediate, and irreversible.

  • Immediately — you're signed out on every device and your account and content disappear from BeReel for everyone.
  • Within 30 days — if you asked for full erasure by email, your data is removed from our database and media storage, and we reply to confirm.

Deletion cannot be undone. There is no grace period and no restore. If you want your content, save it before you delete — you won't be able to get it back afterwards.

06 — Questions

Ask us before you delete, not after.

If you're unsure what deleting removes, or you want to check whether something can be resolved another way, write to admin@bereel.app first. We'll answer.

See also our Privacy Policy for how we handle your information while your account is active.
